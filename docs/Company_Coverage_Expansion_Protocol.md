# Fixed additional-company acquisition protocol

Frozen before additional provider requests, 9 November 2026.

Acquire annual histories for AAPL, MSFT, JNJ, PG, PEP, UNP, HD and MCD.
These are a convenience sample of large US nonfinancial issuers present in the
workbook, not a representative universe or a sample chosen from scored outcomes.
Existing CAT, CVX and NEE inputs remain retained; XOM's rejected identity is not
overridden. Do not substitute companies after inspecting outcome performance.

Expected identities come from exact workbook matches and are retained only in
the private selection manifest. Provider profile must match symbol and ISIN,
be USD, non-ADR and active. Such agreement is only a candidate cross-check,
not independent identity approval or original-publication-vintage evidence.

Fetch profile plus ten annual income, cash-flow and balance-sheet statements,
sequentially (maximum 32 provider calls for eight valid cases). No application
data, approvals, monthly collection schedule or production database is changed.
Keep complete source responses privately, content-addressed. A failure excludes
that case with an explicit reason; no fabricated or substituted observations.

Then run the existing completed three-year FCFE outcome audit on the retained
histories. Count available/excluded outcomes, but do not report that audit as
the D8 comparison: historical predictions, paired projection signals, common
origins, approval and source/currency evidence are separate requirements.
